## JetBrains Mono — `JetBrainsMono-latin.woff2`, `JetBrainsMono-latin-ext.woff2`

JetBrains s.r.o. **SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.** Free to use, modify and
redistribute; may not be sold on its own.
<https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono>

These two files are the `latin` and `latin-ext` subsets as cut by Google Fonts
(v24); the `unicode-range` declarations in `src/styles.css` match those subsets
exactly, so replacing them means updating the ranges too.
